# VASS SEO launch checklist

The website includes crawlable job URLs, page-specific titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, a sitemap, robots rules, LocalBusiness data, and JobPosting data.

These external steps require the business owner's accounts and cannot be completed by publishing code alone:

1. Verify `https://www.vassllc.org` in Google Search Console and submit `https://www.vassllc.org/sitemap.xml`.
2. Claim or update the Virginia Autism Spectrum Services Google Business Profile. Use the same business name, phone, website, Harrisonburg address, and Woodstock address shown on the site.
3. Add the sitemap in Bing Webmaster Tools.
4. Keep business details consistent on trusted healthcare, autism-services, and local chamber directories. Do not buy links or create misleading listings.
5. Review each job page when a role closes. Remove its `JobPosting` markup and sitemap URL when the position is no longer open.
